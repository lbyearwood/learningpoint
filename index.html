Workbook content, quizzes and progress in one simple place.

LearningPoint is a teaching and learning platform for students to access published workbook pages, complete quizzes and view their own scores.

Sign in Preview student dashboard

What students will do

  • Log in using their student account.
  • Open published workbooks and quizzes.
  • Submit quiz answers securely.
  • View their own scores and recent attempts.
